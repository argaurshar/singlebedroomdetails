    Dimensions, assumptions & how this drawing works ＋

    The demonstration room

    3,600 × 4,800 mm clear floor; 3,000 mm finished height. Bed: 1,600 × 2,000 mm. Wardrobe: 2,400 × 600 × 2,400 mm. The original wardrobe-to-bed gap is 1,500 mm. A 450 mm drawer extension leaves 1,050 mm.

    The east door is 850 × 2,100 mm. The north window is 1,500 × 1,200 mm with a 1,200 mm sill. Openings, finishes and fixture ratings are demo assumptions.

    Illustration, with honest numbers

    Physical dimensions and measurements share one model. Cutaway walls and exploded display offsets do not change the room area or volume. The ceiling panel represents a selected portion of the ceiling.

    Light pools and recirculating air traces are schematic. No lux, cooling load, ventilation rate, price or compliance result is implied. The illustrative connected lighting load is 30 W.

    Made to be explored

    Tab to a control; Enter or Space activates its button. Arrow keys adjust focused sliders. Manual waits for each step. Auto follows a 36-second tour; reduced motion shows still states without looping. Reset retains your chosen mode and finish.
